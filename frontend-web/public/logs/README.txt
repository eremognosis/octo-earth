Octo-Earth compliance logs
==========================

These files are deliberately separate from telemetry. They are audit artifacts,
not a substitute for the physics core. A status of NOT_IMPLEMENTED or FAIL
must never be presented as a hackathon pass.

Files:
  validation_benchmarks.csv  Guide benchmark coverage and measured status
  conservation_ledger.csv   External force/reaction accounting
  angular_momentum.csv       Angular-momentum drift record
  habitability.csv           Climate and life-safety record
  convergence.csv            h versus h/2 comparison
  enabler_requirements.csv   bounded enablers and expected failure modes
