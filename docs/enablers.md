# Declared hypothetical enablers

The enablers are assumptions treated as true inside the simulation. They are
bounded capabilities, not hidden changes to the equations and not claims that
the technology exists.

## 1. ACREC

**Antimatter-Catalyzed Relativistic Energy Core** assumes controlled
matter-antimatter annihilation with a mission-scale supply and conversion
system. The audit uses `E = mc^2` for annihilation energy and reports the
required orbital energy, equivalent antimatter fuel mass, and average power.
The antimatter reaction itself is known physics; the bounded, safe,
efficient, mission-scale system is the hypothetical part.

## 2. NMEVA

**Nonlocal Momentum-Exchange Vectoring Array** assumes an external momentum
reservoir or reaction path beyond the Earth system. The model records thrust,
equal/opposite reaction force, and external power instead of pretending that
internal electromagnetic forces can change the center-of-mass momentum of an
isolated system.

## 3. AGCAHS

**Adaptive Geodesic Collision-Avoidance Habitat Shield** assumes a field and
structure envelope that can reject finite-body contact, preserve the habitat,
and maintain the thermal, atmospheric, and magnetic margins. In the core it
performs bounded radius-aware contact resolution and removes closing normal
velocity. Without all three enablers, the protection path is disabled and
collisions or mission-failure status remain visible.

The names are intentionally descriptive. They do not add a fourth enabler:
each is one declared assumption with several directly related capabilities.
